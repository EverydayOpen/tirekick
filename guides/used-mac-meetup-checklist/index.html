Used-Mac meetup checklist

Ten minutes at the meetup, in order of what can cost you most. Tick the boxes on your phone, or print it (the site's menus don't print). Nothing you tick is saved.

Walk away if any of these is true

  • Activation Lock is Enabled and the seller can't turn it off in front of you.
  • The Mac is managed by an organization, or assigned to one in Apple Business or Apple School Manager.
  • Setup shows a Remote Management screen, or asks for someone else's Apple Account.
  • The SSD's S.M.A.R.T. status says anything bad.
